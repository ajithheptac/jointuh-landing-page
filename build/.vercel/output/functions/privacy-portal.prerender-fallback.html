Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

At Jointuh, we believe your files and your activity belong to you.

Jointuh is designed as a privacy-focused download application. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect or sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jointuh handles, how it is used, and your choices regarding your information.

1Information We Collect

Personal Information

Jointuh does not require you to provide personal information to use the application.

We do not require:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Account credentials
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal media
  • Payment information

You can use Jointuh without creating an account.

Download Information

Jointuh may process information necessary to perform downloads and manage files on your device.

This can include:

  • Torrent metadata
  • Magnet links
  • File names
  • File sizes
  • Download and upload progress
  • Peer and tracker information
  • Download speed
  • Upload speed
  • Seeding status
  • Download location
  • Application settings

This information is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the functionality of the application.

Jointuh does not use your download history or file information to build a personal advertising profile.

2Files and Storage

Jointuh may require access to device storage or the Android file system in order to download, save, manage, or open files.

Files downloaded using Jointuh remain under your control on your device.

Jointuh does not upload your personal files to our servers.

Jointuh does not scan the contents of your downloaded files for advertising or profiling purposes.

You are responsible for the files you choose to download, store, share, or distribute using the application.

3Network and Peer-to-Peer Connections

Jointuh uses peer-to-peer technologies to download and, when enabled by you, upload or seed files.

When participating in a peer-to-peer network, certain technical information may be visible to other peers or network participants. Depending on the protocol and network, this may include your device's IP address, port information, torrent information, and other connection-related information required for peer-to-peer communication.

This is an inherent characteristic of peer-to-peer networking and is not information that Jointuh can completely prevent from being visible to other participants.

Jointuh does not operate a central service that records your peer-to-peer activity for advertising or user profiling.

4Analytics and Tracking

Jointuh is designed without behavioral tracking.

We do not use your download activity to track your interests, create advertising profiles, or monitor which files you download.

We do not sell personal information or download information to third parties.

If a future version of Jointuh introduces analytics, diagnostics, or other data collection, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when such functionality is introduced.

5Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Jointuh may collect limited technical information when an application crash or serious technical failure occurs, if crash-reporting functionality is enabled.

This information may include:

  • Application version
  • Operating system version
  • Device model
  • Error logs
  • Crash stack traces
  • Basic technical diagnostics

Crash information is used solely to identify, investigate, and fix technical problems and improve application stability.

Crash reports are not intended to contain the contents of your downloaded files.

6Advertising

Jointuh may display advertisements in certain versions of the application.

If advertising services are used, third-party advertising providers may process certain technical information required to deliver and measure advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Jointuh does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Where applicable, advertising and consent settings may be controlled through your device or the relevant advertising provider.

7Third-Party Services

Jointuh may use third-party services for limited technical purposes, such as:

  • Application distribution
  • Crash reporting
  • Infrastructure
  • Payment processing, if applicable
  • Advertising, if applicable

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Jointuh does not authorize third parties to use your personal information for purposes unrelated to providing their services to Jointuh.

8Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Jointuh may request permissions necessary to operate the application.

For example, permissions may be required to:

  • Save downloaded files
  • Access files selected by you
  • Send notifications
  • Run downloads in the background
  • Connect to network resources

Jointuh attempts to request only permissions necessary for the features you choose to use.

You can manage application permissions through your device settings.

9Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by Jointuh.

However, no software, device, network, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because Jointuh is a peer-to-peer application, some network information is inherently exposed to other participants as part of the underlying protocol.

You should understand the risks associated with peer-to-peer networking before using the application.

10Data Retention

Jointuh does not maintain a centralized database containing your personal download history.

Information stored locally by the application, including download metadata, settings, and task information, may remain on your device until you delete it, remove the relevant download, clear application data, or uninstall Jointuh.

Limited technical or crash information, if collected, may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to diagnose technical issues and improve the application.

11Children's Privacy

Jointuh is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Jointuh does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, we do not knowingly maintain profiles of children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12Your Choices

You control how you use Jointuh and what information you provide to the application.

You can:

  • Use Jointuh without creating an account
  • Delete downloaded files
  • Delete download tasks and metadata
  • Disable notifications
  • Manage application permissions
  • Clear the application's stored data
  • Uninstall the application

If a future version provides account-based or server-side data, additional controls will be made available where required by applicable law.

13International Data Transfers

Jointuh is available to users in different countries.

If limited technical information is processed by third-party service providers, that information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own.

Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

14Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Jointuh's functionality, services, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

For significant changes, we may provide additional notice through the application or website where appropriate.

We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
